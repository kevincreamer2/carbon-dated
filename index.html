Survey (rounds 1–5)
Five quick guesses: two percentages, two Carbon date it years and one Pinpoint on a line. Each answer brushes away a layer of the mound. The closer you are, the better the ruin underneath has survived.
⛏
Excavate (rounds 6–10)
Five trenches. Two are Strata: put five finds in order — drag, or tap two to swap. The others are an Intrusive find (spot the one that doesn't belong), a Forgery (spot the fake label) and Pick the pair (tap the two that share a link). The ruin shows how your dig is going: good answers keep it standing tall, misses wear it down.
🔥
The torch
You get 15 seconds for each survey question, 45 for a Strata trench and 30 for the others. When the torch burns out, whatever you've set is locked in.
